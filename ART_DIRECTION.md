# Strike Vector — Art Direction & Visual Design Guide

## 1. Visual Pillars
1. **Readable High-Contrast Sci-Fi Cyberpunk**: High visual legibility across fast movement speeds. Characters and critical hazards feature emissive accents (cyan `#00E5FF`, warning amber `#FF9100`, hostile crimson `#FF1744`).
2. **Distinct Biome Identity**: Every one of the 8 missions must read instantly from silhouette and palette without text labels:
   - *Urban Blackout*: Wet reflective asphalt, neon shop signs, crumbling concrete, dark rain sky.
   - *High-Speed Rail*: Brushed steel train cars, motion-blurred track ties, tunnel lighting bursts, mountain gorge backdrop.
   - *Harbor Assault*: Industrial orange gantry cranes, deep blue water reflections, shipping containers (teal, red, navy), fog.
   - *Desert Convoy*: Ochre dust plumes, sandstone cliffs, heat distortion, rusted refinery pipelines.
   - *Arctic Installation*: Glacial ice blues, blowing snow blizzard particles, stark white research modules, amber floodlights.
   - *Megafactory*: Molten iron glow, gunmetal assembly arms, warning striping (yellow/black), sparks.
   - *Sky Fortress*: High-altitude azure skies, voluminous cloud banks below, brushed titanium plating, jet exhaust.
   - *Final Citadel*: Brutalist obsidian monoliths, glowing red energy conduits, searchlights, storm atmosphere.
3. **PBR Material Standards**:
   - Albedo color calibrated to real-world reflectances.
   - Metallic and Roughness channels tightly authored to separate matte concrete/rubber from glossy painted steel and burnished alloys.
   - Emission maps for scopes, weapon indicators, enemy optics, and interactive consoles.
4. **VFX & Animation Feedback**:
   - Muzzle flashes with point-light flashes.
   - High-velocity luminous tracer rounds.
   - Impact sparks on metal, dust on concrete, debris on stone.
   - Dynamic camera shake with user-configurable dampening.
