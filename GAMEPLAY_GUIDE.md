# VoltArena — Comprehensive Gameplay & Strategy Guide

## 1. Game 1: Iron Crucible (Tactical Arena FPS)

### 1.1 Weapons Arsenal & Strategy
* **Weapon 1: Pulse Rifle (`Key 1`)**
  * *Role*: All-purpose medium-range kinetic rifle.
  * *Stats*: 12 damage/shot, 30-round clip, 120 reserve, 0.1s fire rate.
  * *Tactic*: Fire in 3-5 round bursts to maintain pinpoint accuracy and prevent spread blooming.
* **Weapon 2: Scatter Cannon (`Key 2`)**
  * *Role*: Devastating close-quarters buckshot.
  * *Stats*: 8 pellets $\times$ 11 damage (88 max), 8-shell tube, 32 reserve.
  * *Tactic*: Ambush bots around blind corners or jump down from upper catwalks for instant lethal point-blank blasts.
* **Weapon 3: Rail Driver (`Key 3`)**
  * *Role*: Long-range sniper beam.
  * *Stats*: 85 damage/shot (170 headshot crit), 5-slug magazine, 20 reserve.
  * *Tactic*: Target enemies on distant platforms. A headshot eliminates bots in a single clean shot.
* **Weapon 4: Grenade Launcher (`Key 4`)**
  * *Role*: Area-denial ballistic explosives.
  * *Stats*: 95 direct damage, 5.0m blast radius with quadratic distance falloff.
  * *Tactic*: Bounce grenades off corridor walls and into bottlenecks to clear defending bots without exposing yourself.
* **Weapon 5: Plasma Cutter (`Key 5`)**
  * *Role*: High-frequency continuous energy beam.
  * *Stats*: 6 damage per tick (30 damage/sec), thermal overheat meter.
  * *Tactic*: Track agile targets smoothly; monitor heat gauge to avoid locking up your weapon during critical moments.

### 1.2 Arena Navigation & Pickups
* **Jump Pads**: Step on neon cyan circular pads for an immediate vertical boost onto high catwalks.
* **Health Pickups**: Glowing green cubes (+25 HP, 15s respawn).
* **Armor Pickups**: Glowing blue shields (+25 Armor, 20s respawn).
* **Ammo Crates**: Glowing yellow crates (Restores all reserve ammo, 25s respawn).

## 2. Game 2: Metro Siege (Subway Wave Survival FPS)

### 2.1 Enemy Archetypes & Behavior
* **Crawler (Red Fast Swarmer)**
  * *Stats*: 120 HP, 8.0 m/s speed, 12 damage.
  * *Behavior*: Swarms in packs; crouches low and leaps forward when within 4 meters.
  * *Counter*: Use the Scatter Cannon or Pulse Rifle to eliminate them before they close the distance.
* **Stalker (Cyan Agile Predator)**
  * *Stats*: 180 HP, 6.5 m/s speed, 22 damage.
  * *Behavior*: Flanks using tunnel darkness; dodges player crosshairs.
  * *Counter*: Use Rail Driver or Plasma Cutter to track rapid erratic movement.
* **Brute (Magenta Heavy Juggernaut)**
  * *Stats*: 450 HP, 3.8 m/s speed, 40 damage.
  * *Behavior*: Slow advance; unleashes an earth-shattering ground slam shockwave.
  * *Counter*: Backpedal continuously, lay down Grenade Launcher fire, and keep at least 5 meters distance.

### 2.2 Tactical Intermission Strategy
Between waves, a 10-second countdown intermission activates:
* Sprint immediately across passenger platforms to collect newly spawned ammo and health crates.
* Reposition yourself near the center platform where sightlines allow you to spot incoming tunnels from all angles.

## 3. Game 3: Nitro Kick (Rocket-Car Football)

### 3.1 Advanced Vehicle Handling
* **Double Jump**: Press `Space` once to launch your car into the air; press `Space` a second time to execute a high vertical leap.
* **Air Orientation**: While airborne, use `W/S` to pitch the vehicle nose up or down, and `A/D` to roll or yaw.
* **Nitrous Boost**: Hold `Shift` (or Right Mouse Button) to engage thrusters, accelerating from 24 m/s up to 38 m/s.

### 3.2 Ball Strike Techniques
* **Power Shot**: Engage nitrous boost right before colliding with the ball to transfer maximum kinetic impulse toward the opponent goal.
* **Wall Bounce**: Drive up the curved corner ramps to intercept high bouncing balls before they reach your defensive zone.
* **Goalkeeping**: Position your car in the mouth of your goal net. When the ball approaches, double jump and boost upward to block incoming aerial shots.

## 4. Game 4: Drift Storm (Arcade Kart Racing)

### 4.1 The 3-Tier Drift Boost Mechanic
Initiate a drift by turning into a corner and pressing `Space` (or Left Mouse Button):
1. **Tier 1 (Blue Sparks)**: Accumulate 1.0 second of sustained drift $\to$ releases $1.2\times$ acceleration surge.
2. **Tier 2 (Orange Sparks)**: Accumulate 2.0 seconds of sustained drift $\to$ releases $1.5\times$ boost.
3. **Tier 3 (Purple Sparks)**: Accumulate 3.2 seconds of sustained drift $\to$ releases massive $1.9\times$ supersonic burst!

### 4.2 Tactical Powerups
* **Turbo Boost**: Instant 3-second top-speed surge.
* **Plasma Shield**: Blocks the next projectile or hazard for 5 seconds.
* **Seeker Missile**: Fires a homing kinetic missile that tracks the leading kart ahead of you.
* **EMP Mine**: Drops an explosive static mine behind your kart; spinning out trailing racers who hit it.
