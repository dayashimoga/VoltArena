## Executive Summary
A forensic runtime and static gap analysis was initiated following the discovery that packaged-runtime builds exhibited critical visual and gameplay defects despite previous automated test passes (1493/1493 headless assertions). The investigation proved that existing tests suffered from false-positive blind spots (testing headless data structures without asserting visual coordinate orientations, bone attachment scales, or rendered world geometry scale). 

Every root cause has been isolated, re-engineered from the foundational transform/rig architecture, and subjected to a dedicated acceptance test suite ([`test_strike_visual_invariants.gd`](file:///h:/gamesmodern/games/strike-vector/tests/test_strike_visual_invariants.gd)).

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **GAP-01** | Operative visually faced and ran backward relative to camera and travel direction. | In `strike_player.gd`, camera look direction was negated incorrectly during velocity projection, and visual mesh rotation was inverted relative to Godot standard forward axis ($-Z$). Additionally, backward animations were chosen when pressing $W$. | 1. Rebuilt input mapping: $W \rightarrow +cam\_fwd$, $S \rightarrow -cam\_fwd$, $D \rightarrow +cam\_right$, $A \rightarrow -cam\_right$.<br>2. Normalized directional animation selector in `StrikePlayerVisual`. | Verified $W$ produces $+cam\_fwd$ travel with facing yaw $\approx 0$ radians (facing $-Z$). Confirmed operative visibly advances forward without moonwalking or backward facing. |
| **GAP-02** | Weapon attached under operative's feet/ankles at world origin `(0, 0, 0)`. | 1. In `soldier.glb`, Mixamo animations (`Aim`, `Fire`, `Reload`, etc.) had position tracks authored in meter units $(0, 0.91, 0)$ instead of centimeters, causing the skeleton to collapse into the floor when played on rigs imported with $0.01$ scale.<br>2. Enforced invariant parenting: all 9 arsenal weapons parent to `WeaponGrip` with `position = ZERO, rotation = ZERO`. | Invariant assertion: Right-hand bone and weapon grip height $\ge 0.85\text{m}$ across all animations. Standardized 5 socket markers on `StrikeWeaponBase`.<br>2. Added `StrikeTacticalMap` (modal overlay toggled via $M$ key) displaying mission corridor progression. | Verified compass bearing dynamically tracks objective angle; minimap blips fade after 3.0s (no omniscient wallhack). |
